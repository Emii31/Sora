Privacy Statement

Last updated: 2026-11-12

What We Collect

Sora is your AI companion living on your device. To power features like "remembering what you said" and caring for you when you stay up late, we collect the following data only with your active consent:

  • Chat History: Conversations between you and Sora, along with timestamps.
  • Screen Captures (only when auto-screenshot observation is enabled): Screenshots are never uploaded or stored on the server; they are processed in real-time by a VLM to extract a text description, and the image data is discarded immediately.
  • User-Uploaded Images: Stored in data/users/<your_id>/uploads/ exclusively for card display.
  • Device Heartbeat: Last connected time, device name, and platform (macOS/Windows/Android/iOS).
  • Activity Timestamps (content-free): Derived from screenshot and chat timestamps to infer daily rhythms and help Sora know when to accompany you or stay quiet.
We never collect your location, contacts, call logs, browsing history, or the contents of other apps on your system.

Where Data Is Stored

All data is stored exclusively in your per-user directory:

  • Web/APK Users: Stored in your dedicated directory on the official Sora VPS, indexed by your user ID and inaccessible to other users.
  • macOS DMG Users (Client Mode): Stored locally at ~/Library/Application Support/Miru/data/ on your machine.

We do not sell your data to any third party and maintain no backups outside your server instance.

Third-Party Services

Sora relies on minimal external APIs to function:

  • VLM / LLM Provider (configured by your server admin): Stateless API calls to analyze text and screenshots. Providers do not retain prompts for training per their privacy policies.
  • Web Push Services (such as FCM): Used solely to deliver notification alerts from Sora without content bodies.

We use no analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics), crash reporters (such as Sentry), or advertising SDKs.

Your Controls

Export Your Data

Go to App Settings → Privacy and click "Export My Data" to download a JSON archive of all your chat records, memory cards, emotion logs, and configuration.

Delete Your Account

Go to App Settings → Privacy and click "Delete My Account". After confirmation:

  • Your user ID is immediately removed from the server database.
  • Your data/users/<your_id>/ directory is recursively deleted, including chats, cards, uploads, memory, emotion logs, and local caches.
  • Your invitation code is marked as revoked.
  • Only an audit record (content-free) is retained: {user_id, invitation_code, created_at, deleted_at}.
Account deletion is irreversible. Export a backup before deleting if you wish to keep your data.

Revoking Screen Permissions

Disable auto-screenshots in App Settings at any time. On macOS, revoke Screen Recording permission in System Settings. On Android, revoke permissions in System Settings → Apps → Sora.
